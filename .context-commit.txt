feat(serve): acceptedEmails lets a serve hand out catch-all addresses (CATCHALL-SERVE-OPTION-1013)

serve-next, candidates/next and candidates/{id}/reveal accept
`acceptedEmails: "valid_only" | "valid_and_catch_all"`. Absent = valid only,
byte-identical body. With valid_and_catch_all a catch_all verdict is served
(unknown/invalid/risky never), and each audience first re-reaches the
catch-all people it already revealed and dropped for the brand, rebuilt from
the stored serve + teaser: no reveal, no verification, no charge, once per
(org, brand, address) via the catch_all_rereaches ledger (migration 0045).
Stating the option adds top-level `emailVerdict` to every served answer.
